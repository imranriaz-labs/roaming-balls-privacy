Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 November 2026Platform: Android / Google Play

How information is handled in Roaming Balls, including the closed-testing release.

This policy applies to Roaming Balls. It does not describe unrelated websites or apps operated under other brands.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Roaming Balls is an Android rolling-ball game published by Games Only Production (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play Roaming Balls, contact us about the game, or view this policy. It applies to the Google Play closed-testing release and will be updated if the game’s features or data practices change.

This policy covers information we control in connection with the game. Android, Google Play, your device manufacturer, and the service used to send us email may process information independently under their own privacy policies.

2. Information handled during gameplay

The current closed-testing version is designed to be playable without creating an account or providing a name, email address, telephone number, or other contact information. It does not include in-game advertising, real-money purchases, social messaging, or a player-operated online service.

Game state may be stored locally on your device, including level progression, earned virtual coins or other in-game rewards, unlocks and gameplay preferences where applicable. This on-device state is used to maintain the game experience. The game is not designed to upload this local progress to a Games Only Production server.

The game does not require access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photographs, or messages for its core gameplay. We do not intentionally request advertising IDs for ad targeting or use in-game advertising or analytics SDKs in this testing release.

3. Google Play and device diagnostics

Google Play, Android, and device-level services may independently process information associated with installation, distribution, app integrity, performance, crashes, security, and device compatibility. Their handling is governed by their respective privacy notices and settings. Depending on Play Console services, Games Only Production may receive aggregated install, performance, or crash information to help diagnose issues and improve reliability; we do not use this information to identify or profile individual players.

For information about Google’s data practices, please consult the Google Privacy Policy at Google Privacy Policy. Our Google Play Data safety answers will describe applicable data collection and sharing for the final release configuration.

4. Information you choose to send us

If you email us about support, feedback, an issue, or privacy rights, we may receive your email address, any name you provide, the content of your message, and any attachments or diagnostic details you voluntarily include. We use this information to respond to your inquiry, investigate an issue, maintain records necessary for security or compliance, and resolve the request. Please do not send passwords, financial information, identity documents, or information about a child unless strictly necessary.

Your email provider and our email service provider may process communications in order to deliver and store messages. We do not use support messages for behavioral advertising or sell them to data brokers.

5. Sharing, selling, and advertising

We do not sell player personal information. The closed-testing release does not display banner, interstitial, or rewarded advertisements and does not use advertising networks to target players.

We may share information from a support request with service providers needed to operate our communication or support systems, or disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect rights, investigate abuse, or respond to lawful requests. No game progress is intentionally shared with third-party players through a built-in messaging feature.

6. Children’s privacy

Roaming Balls is intended for players aged 9 and above, including children aged 9–12, teenagers, and adults. The closed-testing release is designed without accounts, behavioral advertising, in-game messaging, or direct requests for a child’s personal information.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through gameplay. A parent or guardian who believes a child has shared personal information with us through a support message may contact the privacy address below to request review and deletion, subject to applicable legal obligations. If the game later adds online services, advertising, or data collection, we will reassess our children's privacy controls and update this policy before release.

7. Retention and deletion

Game progress and preferences stored only on your device remain there until overwritten or removed by the app or device operating system. You can generally remove locally stored game information through Android app storage controls or by uninstalling the game; doing so may permanently erase progress and unlocks. Because this information is not held in a player account on our servers, we ordinarily cannot retrieve or restore it.

Support emails and privacy requests are retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond, resolve related issues, maintain security records, and meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information held by us, where applicable. We will assess and respond under the laws that apply to your request.

8. Security and international use

We use a data-minimization approach and rely on Android’s application isolation and appropriate controls for information we manage. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Email is not necessarily end-to-end encrypted; avoid sharing sensitive information in unsolicited support messages.

The game may be available internationally through Google Play. Support communications may be processed in countries where our communication service providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards and legal requirements.

9. This policy webpage and external links

This policy is published as a public, read-only webpage through GitHub Pages. When you visit this webpage, GitHub may process technical web-request information in accordance with its own privacy practices. The policy webpage itself contains no advertising or third-party tracking scripts.

External websites linked from this policy are operated by their respective providers. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the app’s functionality, service providers, or handling of information materially changes, and will revise the effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the app and/or Google Play disclosures as appropriate. Please review this page periodically.
